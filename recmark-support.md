# RecMark Support

Welcome to the RecMark support page. RecMark is a native iPhone and iPad app for recreational athletes — it keeps your league schedule, reminds you before every game, and turns each game you log into your own history of stats, records and highlights — synced across your own devices via iCloud.

If you have a question that isn't answered below, please email **mission-sessile.5z@icloud.com** — the developer reads every message personally.

## Contact

- **Email:** mission-sessile.5z@icloud.com
- **Response time:** Typically within 1–2 business days. Critical issues (data loss, sync failures, security) are prioritized.
- **What to include in a support email:** Your app version (Settings → About), the device(s) involved, and as detailed a description as you can manage of what you were doing when the issue occurred. Screenshots are very welcome — please leave out anything personal you'd rather not share. TestFlight testers can also send feedback straight from the app with a screenshot.

## Getting Started

### 1. Choose your sport and your team

When you first open RecMark it asks which sport you play and what your team is called. Give it your jersey number and position too; they're used for every game you log. RecMark supports ice and roller hockey, soccer, basketball, baseball, softball, football, flag football, volleyball, lacrosse, field hockey, rugby, ultimate, tennis and pickleball — or a sport you set up yourself. Add more teams any time, in any sport.

### 2. Get your schedule in

You rarely want to type a season by hand, so there are several ways in:

- **Import a schedule** — a league PDF, one or more screenshots from the group chat, a photo of a printed page, a spreadsheet (CSV or Excel), a calendar file (.ics), or text you paste.
- **Scan it** — point the camera at a printed schedule.
- **Share it in** — share a web page from Safari, or a file from Files or Mail, to RecMark with the share sheet.
- **Describe it** — type something like *"Wednesdays at 9:15 against the Yetis for 8 weeks"*.
- **Add a game** — add a single game by hand any time.

Everything RecMark reads from a file, a picture or a web page is read **on your device**. Nothing is uploaded.

### 3. Say whether you're playing

Each game can be marked **Playing** or **Not playing** in a tap — in the app, from a widget, from Control Center, or with Siri. Reminders follow your answer.

### 4. Log your games

After a game, RecMark asks how it went. Enter the score and your own stats — or leave any of them out. Your history, records and milestones build from there.

### 5. Turn on iCloud (recommended)

If you're signed in to iCloud, your data syncs automatically across your own iPhone and iPad. There's nothing to configure and no account to create. Settings shows whether RecMark is syncing.

## Importing a Schedule

**Does RecMark add games without asking?**
No. Every import opens a review first. Anything RecMark isn't sure about — a team it can't tell apart, a time that might be a score, a venue code — is flagged for you to check before you add the games.

**My league's schedule lists every team. How does RecMark know which games are mine?**
Choose your team when RecMark asks. Team names are matched exactly, so "Bears" and "Bad News Bears" stay different teams. If your league writes your team another way, add that name to the team's **Also Listed As** list so imports find your games automatically.

**The league changed a game. What happens when I import the new schedule?**
RecMark matches the new schedule to the games you already have and shows exactly what changed — new times, places and cancellations — before anything is updated. Your answers, stats, notes and photos stay with each game.

**I imported a schedule partway through the season.**
Games that are already over are never assumed. RecMark asks whether you played them, and **Catch Up** walks you through adding their results whenever you're ready.

**Can I undo an import?**
Yes. Each import is kept as a record, and undoing it takes out the games it added and puts back what it changed.

**What about a Numbers spreadsheet?**
RecMark reads a Numbers file from its preview. For the best result, export it to CSV or Excel from Numbers first.

## Game Day and Reminders

- Reminders arrive before every game you're playing, with a nudge to log it afterwards. Choose when in Settings → Reminders, or per team.
- If you're a substitute and haven't said yet, RecMark asks "Are you playing?" instead.
- The Home tab shows your next game, any games waiting to be logged, your last result and your season so far.
- **Directions** opens Apple Maps to the venue, and your notes for the game are right there.

**I'm not getting game reminders.**
Check that notifications are allowed for RecMark (Settings → Notifications → RecMark) and that reminders are on in RecMark's Settings. Games you've marked **Not playing** don't remind you.

## Logging a Game

- Each sport has its own stats: hockey skaters and goalies, soccer, basketball and the rest. Set-by-set scores and doubles partners are there for tennis, pickleball and volleyball.
- **Only what you record counts.** Anything you leave out stays unknown — it is never turned into a zero, so your averages stay honest. A game you didn't play never adds a zero line to your stats, and cancelled or postponed games never count.
- If your league doesn't keep a stat, turn it off for that team and it stops asking.
- Add notes, highlights and photos to any game.

## Stats, Records and Milestones

- Career and season stats for every team and sport, personal records, streaks and milestones — celebrated when they happen.
- Your record against every opponent.
- A **Season in Review** when the season ends, and share cards for your best moments.

## Playoffs

When your league's schedule includes a playoff bracket, RecMark keeps it as the league wrote it. Tell RecMark whether your team made it, and its seed, and the playoff games that are yours fill in as the bracket does.

## Calendar

RecMark can add your games to your calendar so they show up beside everything else in your week.

- Add a single game, a season, or a whole team — or turn on **Keep games in Calendar** in Settings.
- Choose where they go: a calendar just for your games, or one of your own.
- When a time or a place changes, the events follow. Cancelled games leave the calendar; postponed games stay, marked "Postponed".
- Notes you write on a game's event in Calendar come back to the game. A new time or place set in Calendar is offered to you, never applied on its own.
- Tapping the link in a game's event opens that game in RecMark.
- RecMark only ever changes events it added. **Remove RecMark Events** in Settings takes them all out again.

## Siri and Shortcuts

RecMark is built to be used by voice. You can say things like:

- *"When is my next game in RecMark"*
- *"I'm playing in RecMark"*
- *"I'm not playing in RecMark"*
- *"Log my game in RecMark"*
- *"Log my stats in RecMark"*
- *"Add a game in RecMark"*
- *"Show my season stats in RecMark"*
- *"Show my recent games in RecMark"*
- *"Open my last game in RecMark"*
- *"Ask RecMark"*

RecMark always asks you to confirm before it changes anything. Every one of these also works as a Shortcut you can build on, and Spotlight finds your games by opponent, venue or date.

## Widgets and Controls

- **Next Game**, **Upcoming Games**, **Season Stats** and **Last Game** widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen, each set to the team (and season) you choose.
- **Control Center** controls to say you're playing in your next game, or to log a game, without opening the app.

## Ask RecMark

Ask about your history in your own words — *"How many goals did I score last season?"* or *"My record against the Bears"* — and RecMark answers from your own games. **Describe a Game** works the same way for adding games: type what you'd tell a teammate, and RecMark shows you exactly what it understood before anything is saved.

This uses Apple Intelligence on your device where it's available, to help with unusual wording. Where it isn't, RecMark reads your words itself, in every language it speaks, and everything still works.

## Your Data

Settings → **Your Data** holds everything for keeping and moving your history:

- **Export Everything** makes a RecMark archive (.recmark) — every team, season, game, stat, note and picture — that you can keep anywhere.
- **Restore from Archive** brings an archive back. It lands all at once or not at all, so a half-finished restore can never leave your data in a strange state.
- **Export Games & Stats (CSV)** makes a spreadsheet of your games and stats.
- **Clean Up Duplicates** merges any copies of the same game, season or team, keeping everything you entered.

## iCloud Sync

**How do my iPhone and iPad stay in step?**
Through your private iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on both and make sure iCloud is on for RecMark (Settings → \[Your Name\] → iCloud → Saved to iCloud → See All). RecMark's Settings show whether it is syncing or saving on this device only.

**A change hasn't reached my other device yet.**
iCloud can take a few minutes, longer on a poor connection or in Low Power Mode. Open RecMark on both devices while connected to the internet. Nothing is lost while you wait: each device keeps its changes until iCloud delivers them.

**I see the same game twice.**
RecMark merges copies of the same record automatically as soon as a device sees them. If a duplicate remains, Settings → Your Data → **Clean Up Duplicates** runs the same merge, which keeps everything you entered.

## Accessibility

RecMark is built to work with VoiceOver, Dynamic Type including the largest accessibility sizes, Reduce Motion, and Increase Contrast. Results are never shown by colour alone — every win, loss and tie says so in words. If you hit something that doesn't work well with an accessibility setting you rely on, please email; those reports are prioritized.

## Languages

RecMark is available in English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified Chinese, Spanish and Traditional Chinese. Describe a Game and Ask RecMark understand all eleven.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there an Android version?**
No. RecMark is built natively with SwiftUI and SwiftData for the Apple ecosystem only.

**Is there a Mac, Apple Watch or web version?**
Not today. RecMark is an iPhone and iPad app, and stores all data on-device and in your private iCloud. Widgets, Control Center and Siri cover the quick moments.

**Is RecMark for team managers?**
No — it's built for the player. It keeps your own history, across every team and every sport you play. It doesn't manage a roster or message your team.

**Does it need an internet connection?**
Only for iCloud sync, which is Apple's. The app itself makes no network requests — there is no server, no account, and nothing to sign up for.

**Can I use RecMark without iCloud?**
Yes. Everything works on a single device. You won't get sync across your devices, and nothing else changes.

**Does it work without Apple Intelligence?**
Yes. Every feature works without it. On a device that can run Apple Intelligence, RecMark's Settings show whether it's helping, and why not if it isn't.

**Does it read my other calendars?**
It never stores, copies or changes your other events. It looks only for the events it added itself, by the link back to the game written into each one.

**Will my data survive an app update?**
Yes. SwiftData and CloudKit carry your data forward across updates. Use Settings → Your Data → Export Everything if you want an additional copy at any time.

**How do I delete all my data?**
Delete games, seasons or teams in the app. To remove everything RecMark keeps in iCloud, go to Settings → \[Your Name\] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → RecMark → Delete Data, and delete the app from your devices. See the [Privacy Policy](recmark-privacy-policy.md) for full details.

**I found a bug. Where do I report it?**
Email **mission-sessile.5z@icloud.com** with your app version and what you were doing, or [open an issue](../../issues). Screenshots help enormously.

**I have a feature request. Where do I send it?**
Same email: **mission-sessile.5z@icloud.com**. Feature requests are read and prioritized for upcoming releases.
